# POL-265 — Commercial Approvals — Operating Standard (POL-265) (Confidential — Manager Access)

Policy ID: POL-265

Owner: Renewal Operations

Effective: 2026-07-15

Last reviewed: 2026-07-29

Status: Superseded — historical reference only

Access: Manager Access



## Scope
Applies to renewal requests involving renewal concession for customers on standard and enterprise plans.

## Required handling
The assigned support rep must validate the account ID, capture the incident or invoice reference, and document the action in the support ticket. Initial acknowledgment target is 8 business hours.

## Approval boundaries
Support reps may issue customer credits up to $250 when eligible. Manager approval is required for amounts above $250 and up to $5,000. Director review is required above $5,000. The $7,500 figure in this section is an example review threshold for renewal exceptions, not a blanket authorization.

## Exception
For repeat renewal concession events, attach the latest technical or billing evidence. Do not promise a timeline or concession before the owning team confirms feasibility.

## Audit trail
Record the ticket ID, affected account, policy ID POL-265, approver, decision, and date. When sources disagree, use the newest active policy and escalate ambiguity.

## Related guidance
See REV queue playbook and incident postmortem archive.

## Renewal-specific rule
For churn-risk accounts with ARR over $75,000, managers may be pre-authorized to offer up to a 20% renewal discount subject to eligibility review. Multi-year commitments above 15% require director approval.

This policy is superseded. Do not apply it to current customer decisions; use the active policy and record the superseding reference.
