# POL-249 — Integrations Support — Operating Standard (POL-249)

Policy ID: POL-249

Owner: Integrations Operations

Effective: 2026-05-28

Last reviewed: 2026-06-11

Status: Active

Access: Public



## Scope
Applies to integrations requests involving connector sync for customers on standard and enterprise plans.

## Required handling
The assigned support rep must validate the account ID, capture the incident or invoice reference, and document the action in the support ticket. Initial acknowledgment target is 8 business hours.

## Approval boundaries
Support reps may issue customer credits up to $250 when eligible. Manager approval is required for amounts above $250 and up to $5,000. Director review is required above $5,000. The $5,000 figure in this section is an example review threshold for integrations exceptions, not a blanket authorization.

## Exception
For repeat connector sync events, attach the latest technical or billing evidence. Do not promise a timeline or concession before the owning team confirms feasibility.

## Audit trail
Record the ticket ID, affected account, policy ID POL-249, approver, decision, and date. When sources disagree, use the newest active policy and escalate ambiguity.
