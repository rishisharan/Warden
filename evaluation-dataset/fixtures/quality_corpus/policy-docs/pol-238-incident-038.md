# POL-238 — Incident Response — Operating Standard (POL-238)

Policy ID: POL-238

Owner: Incident Operations

Effective: 2026-04-25

Last reviewed: 2026-05-09

Status: Active

Access: Public



## Scope
Applies to incident requests involving service disruption for customers on standard and enterprise plans.

## Required handling
The assigned support rep must validate the account ID, capture the incident or invoice reference, and document the action in the support ticket. Initial acknowledgment target is 24 business hours.

## Approval boundaries
Support reps may issue customer credits up to $250 when eligible. Manager approval is required for amounts above $250 and up to $5,000. Director review is required above $5,000. The $7,500 figure in this section is an example review threshold for incident exceptions, not a blanket authorization.

## Exception
For repeat service disruption events, attach the latest technical or billing evidence. Do not promise a timeline or concession before the owning team confirms feasibility.

## Audit trail
Record the ticket ID, affected account, policy ID POL-238, approver, decision, and date. When sources disagree, use the newest active policy and escalate ambiguity.

## Related guidance
See INC queue playbook and incident postmortem archive.

## SLA-specific rule
Three qualifying incidents in a quarter trigger enterprise SLA review; customer-facing credits require separate approval.
