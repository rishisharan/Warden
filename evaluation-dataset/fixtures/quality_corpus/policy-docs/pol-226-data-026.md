# POL-226 — Data Governance — Operating Standard (POL-226) (Confidential — Manager Access)

Policy ID: POL-226

Owner: Data Operations

Effective: 2026-03-20

Last reviewed: 2026-04-03

Status: Superseded — historical reference only

Access: Manager Access



## Scope
Applies to data requests involving export retention for customers on standard and enterprise plans.

## Required handling
The assigned support rep must validate the account ID, capture the incident or invoice reference, and document the action in the support ticket. Initial acknowledgment target is 24 business hours.

## Approval boundaries
Support reps may issue customer credits up to $250 when eligible. Manager approval is required for amounts above $250 and up to $5,000. Director review is required above $5,000. The $7,500 figure in this section is an example review threshold for data exceptions, not a blanket authorization.

## Exception
For repeat export retention events, attach the latest technical or billing evidence. Do not promise a timeline or concession before the owning team confirms feasibility.

## Audit trail
Record the ticket ID, affected account, policy ID POL-226, approver, decision, and date. When sources disagree, use the newest active policy and escalate ambiguity.
