# POL-275 — Commercial Approvals — Operating Standard (POL-275) (Confidential — Manager Access)

Policy ID: POL-275

Owner: Renewal Operations

Effective: 2026-08-14

Last reviewed: 2026-08-28

Status: Active

Access: Manager Access



## Scope
Applies to renewal requests involving renewal concession for customers on standard and enterprise plans.

## Required handling
The assigned support rep must validate the account ID, capture the incident or invoice reference, and document the action in the support ticket. Initial acknowledgment target is 48 business hours.

## Approval boundaries
Support reps may issue customer credits up to $250 when eligible. Manager approval is required for amounts above $250 and up to $5,000. Director review is required above $5,000. The $250 figure in this section is an example review threshold for renewal exceptions, not a blanket authorization.

## Exception
For repeat renewal concession events, attach the latest technical or billing evidence. Do not promise a timeline or concession before the owning team confirms feasibility.

## Audit trail
Record the ticket ID, affected account, policy ID POL-275, approver, decision, and date. When sources disagree, use the newest active policy and escalate ambiguity.

## Related guidance
See REV queue playbook and incident postmortem archive.

## Renewal-specific rule
For churn-risk accounts with ARR over $75,000, managers may be pre-authorized to offer up to a 20% renewal discount subject to eligibility review. Multi-year commitments above 15% require director approval.
