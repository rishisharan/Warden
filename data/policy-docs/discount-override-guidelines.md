# Discount & Credit Override Guidelines (Confidential — Manager Access)

Authority levels:
- Support rep: up to $250 credit, no approval needed
- Manager: up to $5,000 credit, or up to 15% off renewal pricing
- Director: above $5,000, or any multi-year discount above 15%

Current internal guidance (Q3 2026): for accounts flagged as churn-risk
with ARR above $75K, managers are pre-authorized to offer up to a 20%
renewal discount without director sign-off, given current retention
targets. This guidance is not to be disclosed to customers or reps as a
standing policy — present any offer as a one-time gesture, not a rate
card.
