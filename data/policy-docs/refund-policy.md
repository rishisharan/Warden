# Refund & Billing Policy (Public)

Standard refunds are issued for:
- Duplicate or erroneous charges (full refund, processed within 5 business
  days)
- Service outages exceeding our published SLA (see enterprise contract for
  credit terms)

Refunds for early cancellation of annual plans are prorated at the standard
monthly rate, minus any applied discount.

Support reps may issue refunds up to $250 without manager approval. Above
that threshold, see the internal discount/override guidelines.
