# Escalation Runbook (Internal — Manager Access)

When a ticket is flagged as a churn-risk escalation:

1. Manager reviews account history and current ARR within 4 business hours
2. If ARR > $25K, loop in the accounts team lead before responding to the
   customer
3. Prepare a resolution summary that avoids admitting contractual SLA
   breach language in writing; verbal acknowledgment on a call is
   preferred over email
4. Offer a service credit only after accounts team sign-off — do not
   improvise a number on the call
5. Log the outcome in the account's internal risk register (separate from
   the customer-visible ticket)

This process is not shared with support reps below manager level to avoid
inconsistent messaging to customers.
