# General Support FAQ (Public)

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. If the email doesn't
arrive within 10 minutes, check spam or contact support.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
Latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.

**Q: How do I export my data?**
Account Settings → Data Export. Exports are generated as CSV and emailed
within 24 hours.

**Q: Do you support single sign-on (SSO)?**
Google OAuth is supported today. SAML-based SSO is on the roadmap.
