# Enterprise SLA Terms (Internal — Manager Access)

Applies to all accounts on the Enterprise tier ($50K+ ARR).

- 99.9% uptime commitment, measured monthly
- Service credits: 5% of monthly fee per 0.1% below commitment, capped at
  30% of monthly fee
- Three or more qualifying incidents in a rolling 90-day window triggers a
  mandatory executive review call, per contract section 4.2
- Churn-risk accounts (any account that has triggered the above, or
  explicitly raised cancellation) must be flagged to the accounts team
  within 24 hours

Support reps should NOT quote specific credit percentages or reference
"SLA breach" language directly to customers — route to a manager.
